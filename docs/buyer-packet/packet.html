Mythos AI SecurityIs this relevant to us?

If AI can reach important information or take actions, test the boundaries.

Start with a workflow your business already uses or plans to release. The most useful first assessment has a named owner and a real decision: launch it, add a tool, expand its access or verify a repair.

Ask your teamWhy it matters
Can AI read customer or internal records?A useful answer can still expose the wrong record, person or destination.
Can it send messages or change software or records?A mistaken instruction may become a business action.
Can it act on documents, email or web content?Outside material may contain instructions that conflict with the approved task.
Does it rely on human approval?Approval needs to match the action that is actually sent.
Are you unsure what it can reach?Uncertainty is a reason to map a bounded workflow before expanding access.

Mythos AI SecurityHow does this go beyond a scan?

Connect the task, the permission and the outcome.

The important question is whether a workflow stays within its authority when the inputs, tool results or conversation become misleading. We begin with what your system is supposed to do, then compare that with supported observations.

  • Context and access. Identify the agreed systems, data, identities, tools and model route. Athena provides relevant application and deployment context within the supported scope.
  • Behavior and authority. Achilles challenges the agreed prompt, retrieval, tool, permission and approval boundaries, including relevant multi-turn and cross-channel cases.
  • Action and outcome. Compare the expected allow, escalate, hold or deny decision with the response, execution records and available external evidence.

SPINE connects supported claims, evidence, repair status and the deployment recommendation. Your release owner makes the decision.

An AI saying it performed an action does not prove the action happened. Verified external effects require a witness outside the tested model. Unobserved paths remain visible as gaps.

Standard Achilles scan results do not yet enter the signed platform record. Mythos does not audit identity providers or every user's individual permissions.
